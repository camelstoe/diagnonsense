What is this?

Project Diagnonsense is a daily symptom tracker for people navigating undiagnosed or early-stage inflammatory conditions. It tracks joint temperatures against anatomical controls, ring sizes and circumferences, bilateral pain scores with type detail, neurological symptoms, and syncs everything to a personal Google Sheet.

Your data is yours

Everything goes directly to a Google Sheet you own. No server, no tracking, no advertising.

Temperature protocol

Inner forearm — mid-point wrist to elbow. Upper body control: elbows, hands, wrists.

Calf — mid-calf. Lower body control: knees, hips, ankles, feet.

Forehead — facial control for jaw readings. Measure at the same point each time.
